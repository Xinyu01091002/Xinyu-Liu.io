# Welcome to Xinyu's Personal WebPages

## About

I am Xinyu Liu, currently based in the Department of Engineering Science at the University of Oxford. My research focuses on nonlinear water waves and computational methods for bound-wave reconstruction.

[GitHub](https://github.com/Xinyu01091002) · [ORCID: 0009-0003-2376-8899](https://orcid.org/0009-0003-2376-8899)

## Research Interests

1. Nonlinear water waves and wave interaction theory
2. Efficient reconstruction of bound waves in finite depth and directional seas
3. Spectral methods and computational verification
4. Phase manipulation and harmonic reconstruction

## Research Code

- [Green–Laplace wave reconstruction](https://github.com/Xinyu01091002/green-laplace-wave-reconstruction): MATLAB and Python implementations for finite-depth directional bound-wave reconstruction, with reproduction examples.
- [MF12 wave reconstruction](https://github.com/Xinyu01091002/spectral-domain-implementation-of-wave-interaction-theory): MATLAB, Python and C++ implementations of the third-order Madsen–Fuhrman (2012) framework, including spectral reconstruction and cross-language verification.
- [Phase-combination](https://github.com/Xinyu01091002/phase-combination): wave phase manipulation and harmonic reconstruction scripts, with a shared toolkit for simulation and experimental data.
